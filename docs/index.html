How to subscribe (vs importing)

Subscribe, don't just import. Subscribing keeps the calendar in sync automatically: when fixture times, venues, or (eventually) finals participants change, your calendar updates itself. Importing a one-off copy of an .ics file creates a static snapshot that never changes.

  • Apple Calendar: Calendar → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar → paste the URL. Set auto-refresh to hourly.
  • Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL → paste the URL. Google refreshes roughly every 24 hours.
  • Outlook: Add Calendar → Subscribe from web → paste the URL.

Event durations (AFL 150 minutes, AFLW 120 minutes) are calendar scheduling estimates, not actual match lengths. Times are stored in UTC and shown in your local timezone automatically.

Finals with unconfirmed participants (e.g. “Winner of PF1 v Winner of PF2”) appear in the All Teams feeds, and in club feeds once resolved. Your subscription updates automatically.
